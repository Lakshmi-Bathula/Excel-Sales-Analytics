### 📊 Excel-Sales-Analytics Dashboard
This project focuses on analyzing AtliQ Hardware’s sales data using Excel to understand customer trends and market performance.
It compares sales from 2019 to 2021 to identify growth and track performance across different customers and markets.
The reports also help monitor key sales KPIs and compare actual sales with targets.
The main goal is to turn sales data into useful insights that help the business make better decisions.

 ### 🎯 Objectives
Analyze Customer Performance: Evaluate customer sales and identify key customers contributing to overall sales.
Compare Market Performance with Targets: Compare actual sales with sales targets to identify performance gaps across different markets.

### 📌 Key Insights & Techniques

* Analyzed customer sales from 2019 to 2021.
* Used Pivot Tables to summarize sales data.
* Compared market sales with sales targets.
* Identified sales growth and target gaps.

### 💼 Business Value

This project helps businesses:

* Identify top-performing customers.
* Compare actual sales with sales targets.
* Understand sales growth across different markets.
* Make better decisions using sales data.
